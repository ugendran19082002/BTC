  THE SIZING TRAP
    A zero observed worst day does NOT mean zero risk. You cannot size a naked
    short position off a loss that simply has not occurred yet. Step 1 has a
    measurable -477 worst day and can be sized against it; the full stack
    cannot be sized off its own history at all.

  LOOKAHEAD CHECK
    PCR>median was re-run with an EXPANDING median (only data up to that day).
    Result: INR 1,695 vs 1,709 in-sample. The PCR filter is not an artefact of
    using the whole sample to set its threshold.

  WHAT THIS ACTUALLY SAYS
    Premium/EM <= 0.020 is the single best risk-adjusted addition: it lifts PF
    from 4.81 to 24.41 and cuts the worst day from -677 to -178 while keeping
    475 of 563 trading days. The PCR filter and the 70% exit both buy further
    safety at a steep cost in return and in days deployed.

    If the goal is the most money: step 1 (CE<=15 + PE 15-40), INR 7,916.
    If the goal is the smoothest curve: the full stack, INR 1,709.
