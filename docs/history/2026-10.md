## Entry setups: SL / TP per method, graded live (1 Oct 2026)

The owner's asks of the night of 30 Sep - 1 Oct, in the entry section of the
desk tab ([features/entry-setups.md](../features/entry-setups.md)):

- **SL and targets per method**, from the owner's tables: each method's stop
  is its own structure + 0.25 ATR (the FVG's is now the displacement origin);
  TP1 looks first at the method's own kind of level (a continuation swing, a
  book wall, the OI wall, VWAP for #10), TP2 at its own pool (1H/4H liquidity,
  the next wall, the next OI wall), TP3 at the expected-move edge (max pain for
  options). Every plan says why each level is there.
- **TP1 is the nearest *valid* target**: consumed swings dropped, nearer levels
  that pay under the minimum skipped (and said so). The minimum is **1R** --
  the owner's, over TEST.md's 1.8 -- one number for the choice and the gate.
- **TGT1 / TGT2 / TGT3 graded**: after TP1 a runner, stop at breakeven, is
  watched for TP2 and TP3 (`entry-012`). The record's R stays the TP1 exit.
- **Graded on the live tape**: fills, stops and targets the second they print
  on the perpetual (`entry/live-grade.ts`), 1m candles only as the backstop.
- **missed**: a limit that price ran away from -- to TP1 without filling -- is
  cancelled, not filled late (`entry-013`). The owner's screenshot of a VWAP
  short "waiting for price" with its levels drawn was this.
- **No fee term** in the entry section's R:R and R (`entry-010` recomputed the
  stored R); "after fees" removed from the screen. Delta's charges still come
  off the real P&L report.
- **1m without the chain is chart-only** (`entry-008` removed its signals).
- **Signal history**: TRADING tab, TGT1-3 / Entry / Exit columns with times and
  slippage, every column sortable on the server, totals that add up (net =
  target − SL ± time-out), no Net R, Excel download, the entry clock and
  counters, refreshed every 5 s.

**What broke, and why.**

- *Part-minute candles.* The candle cache answers stale while it refreshes;
  at 12:05:03 it could return data asked for at 12:04:45, and the 12:04
  minute -- 45 seconds of it -- passed as closed because the filter compared
  with *now*. Graded once, that minute was never read again: stop and TP1
  touches in its last seconds were missed and showed up a minute later as
  "gaps" (19 of 25 live 3m stops). A candle is now closed only if it closed
  before the data was *asked for* (`wholeUntil`), and the engine asks for data
  fetched after the minute turned (`venueSeriesSince`).
- *A zone past its own stop.* Three 1m retest setups had their zone reach
  beyond the stop; the zone is now kept 0.1 ATR clear of it (`zoneOf`).
- *Filters saved before a change* (1m, the R:R column) could leave the history
  filtering by something with no chip to undo it; saved filters are cleaned.
- *A target "passed" on the tab title* (83,672) was the index, ~40 points over
  the perpetual, which never traded above 83,657 there: the paper log was
  right. Checked against Delta's own 1m candles.

**R&D** (5m, Jan-Aug 2026): nearest TP1 -0.21R per trade; nearest valid at
