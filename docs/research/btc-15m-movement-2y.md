# BTC movement by 15-minute slot — 5:35 PM to 5:29 PM IST, last two years

How much BTC moves in each 15 minutes of the desk's trading day, measured over **two years, 730 days** (5 Oct 2024 to 5 Oct 2026). Each day runs from 5:35 PM to 5:29 PM the next afternoon, the same hours a strategy takes signals in, cut into 96 slots.

## What the columns mean

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Time (IST)** | The 15-minute slot. |
| **Avg move %** | How far price ended from where the slot started, on average, ignoring direction. |
| **Avg move pts** | The same in BTC points (dollars), averaged over the two years. |
| **Avg range %** | High minus low inside the slot, on average: the full swing, not only start to end. |
| **Avg range pts** | The same in points. |
| **Up days** | Share of days the slot closed higher than it opened. Near 50% means no lean either way. |
| **Biggest rise / fall** | The largest single up move and down move seen in that slot, in points. |
| **Days** | How many days went into the row. A few are missing where the exchange paused. |

**Points depend on the price.** BTC ranged from 57,766 (1 Jul 2026) to 126,206 (7 Oct 2025) in this period, so the same percentage was a different number of points at different times. To turn a percentage into points at today's price (85,636): multiply — 0.100% is about 86 points.

## The short version

- **A typical 15 minutes:** price ends 0.155% from where it started (about 133 points at today's price), and swings 0.308% high to low (about 264 points).
- **Half of all slots move less than 0.101%** (about 86 points). The average is higher because a few very large moves pull it up.
- **Most active slot:** 8:05 PM – 8:20 PM, 0.286% on average — 2.7 times the quietest.
- **Quietest slot:** 11:20 AM – 11:35 AM, 0.105% on average.
- **Busiest hour:** 7:35 PM – 8:35 PM (0.252% per slot). **Calmest hour:** 10:35 AM – 11:35 AM (0.117% per slot).
- **No slot leans up or down.** Up days run from 46% to 54% across the day, which is what coin-flips would give over 730 days (about 45% to 55%). The time of day tells you how much BTC moves, not which way.
- **The pattern holds across both years.** 9 of the 10 most active slots in the first year are also in the second year's top 10 (rank correlation 0.87, where 1.00 is identical).

## How this was made

- **Source:** Delta Exchange India, BTCUSD perpetual, 5-minute candles from its public history: 210,234 candles.
- **Slots:** three 5-minute candles each, starting at 5:35 PM IST, so a slot runs 5:35–5:50, 5:50–6:05 and so on. The five minutes from 5:30 PM to 5:35 PM (settlement) are not in any slot.
- **Move** is the slot's last price minus its first. **Range** is its highest price minus its lowest.
- **Left out:** 146 slots (of 70,079) that fell in an exchange pause or had a candle missing, so a pause is not counted as a quiet market.
- **Averages include the extreme days.** The crash of 11 Oct 2025 (2:45 AM IST) alone moved price over 8% in five minutes. The "half of all slots" figure above is the one that is not pulled by such days.
- This is a description of the past two years. It measures how much BTC moved at each time of day. It does not test any strategy.
