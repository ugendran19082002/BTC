# Perp SL minimum by timeframe, at each account cap (1 Oct – 1 Nov 2026)

Owner, 2 Nov 2026: "perp SL min 0 to 500, time frame based, profit loss win% in max open 100 … 350 — table kudu". A companion
to [2026-11-rd-profit-and-loss.md](2026-11-rd-profit-and-loss.md) §6, which measured the same minimums without a cap.

**How.** The desk's own backtest, day by day 19 Sep – 1 Nov, on High's live strategies with every method, the SL minimum
off and the $13 floor kept, trade by trade with entry and exit times. Then, per account: the A7 no-entry windows; the
minimum under test; the daily method pick replayed as live (High win 70%, the others 80%, picked every 14 days on the 14
days before); and the trades taken in time order under the cap — at most *cap* open at once, each side at most 80% of it,
each strategy at most 100 — a trade that finds no room is skipped. Medium, Low and Income share every rule now, so the
sum is High plus three times the others. ₹ per contract after charges. Not modelled: the most on one strike (22%, the next
strike out), the desk's real fills (the backtest is optimistic: read the direction, not the rupees).

**Read it this way.** Each "alone" table moves one timeframe's minimum and keeps the others as live (3m 150, 5m 150, 15m
150, all time 100, 30m 200, 1h 250, 2h 250, 4h 300). NET and loss are against the live settings **at the same cap**.
