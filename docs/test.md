Available Funds (USD) = Fund (₹) ÷ USD/INR

உதாரணம்:
₹10,000 ÷ 85 = $117.65

CE

CE Position BTC = CE Lots × Lot Size

CE Margin USD = CE Lots × Margin per Lot

CE Margin ₹ = CE Margin USD × USD/INR

CE Max Gross Profit USD = CE Lots × Lot Size × CE Entry Premium

CE Max Gross Profit ₹ = CE Max Gross Profit USD × USD/INR

Short CE premium $0-க்கு வந்தால் கிடைக்கும் gross premium தான் இந்த theoretical max profit.

PE

அதே formula:

PE Position BTC = PE Lots × Lot Size

PE Margin USD = PE Lots × Margin per Lot

PE Margin ₹ = PE Margin USD × USD/INR

PE Max Gross Profit USD = PE Lots × Lot Size × PE Entry Premium

PE Max Gross Profit ₹ = PE Max Gross Profit USD × USD/INR

CE + PE Combined

Combined Margin USD = CE Margin + PE Margin

Combined Margin ₹ = Combined Margin USD × USD/INR

Remaining Margin USD = Available Funds USD − Combined Margin USD

Remaining Margin ₹ = Remaining Margin USD × USD/INR

Maximum lots

Max Lots = FLOOR(Available Funds USD ÷ Margin per Lot USD)

Example

10 CE + 10 PE, lot size 0.001, margin/lot $0.50, USD/INR 85:

CE margin = 10 × $0.50 = $5 = ₹425

PE margin = 10 × $0.50 = $5 = ₹425

Combined = $10 = ₹850

₹10,000 fund → $117.65 available

Remaining = $117.65 − $10 = $107.65 ≈ ₹9,150
