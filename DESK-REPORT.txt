==========================================================================================================================
  WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SAY
==========================================================================================================================
  - No premium floor and no variant reaches a 100% win rate. The best any
    of them manages is in the high nineties, and the losses that remain are
    the days BTC moved further in twelve hours than the strike was away.
  - The strategy is short a tail. Its win rate is high because most days are
    quiet, and its loss is large because the rare day is not.
  - On the most dangerous days Delta often lists no cheap strike at all, so
    the rule stands aside. Part of the win rate is that filter, not skill.
  - Protection is frequently unlisted at the distances this sells, so a
    bounded worst case is often unavailable. Size is the real risk control.
  - The largest effect in the data is not the strike rule at all. It is the
    calendar: Monday to Friday lost money on 3 days out of 461, worst -$0.95.
    The weekend lost on 7 of 209, worst -$8.48, and holds all six of the
    biggest losses in two years. Every year says the same thing.
  - An earlier version of this analysis said Saturday loses money. On the
    complete data it does not; it makes a little, badly. The case for standing
    aside at the weekend is about where the tail lives, not about the average.
