AND WAITING?
======================================================================================
  P(expire worthless) climbs on its own as the day runs out -- d2 carries
  a 1/sqrt(T), so the same strike that reads 92% at 05:30 will read 95% a
  few hours later provided spot has not moved against it. So yes: a leg
  refused at 05:30 can be taken later, and the day need never be skipped.

  What it costs is the premium, which decays over exactly those hours, and
  the two do not move at the same rate. It is a real strategy and it is
  NOT measurable here: the harvester captures the chain once, at 05:30, so
  there is no later board to re-select a strike from. Adding a second
  snapshot to harvest_chain.py is what would settle it.

  STEP OUT needs no such wait. It takes the extra distance immediately, at
  05:30, out of the same board -- which is why it is the one measured here.
