====================================================================================
WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SETTLE
====================================================================================
  Pricing now matches the market's own shape, so the strike selection is
  defensible in a way the flat-vol sweep was not. What has not changed:
  there are still no recorded option quotes at 23:30 IST. The entry price,
  the exit price and every step between are computed, not observed.

  The day trade needs no such disclaimer. Its entry marks, settle values and
  decay paths are all read from chain.db.

  To settle the overnight question the way the day trade is settled, add a
  23:30 IST snapshot to harvester/harvest_chain.py and wait.
