10 contracts per leg, 5% slippage, 733 days, all measured.

WHY THIS USES A MODEL LINE AND NOT AN OBSERVED-RATE LINE
  "observed rate >= 99%" judges a bucket, and near the top a bucket of 200
  legs flips from 99.0% to 98.5% on one bad leg, taking every leg in it out
  of the strategy. That is why it selected 275 legs calibrated one way and
  7 the other. Model probability is per leg and moves smoothly, and the
  calibration says legs scored 97%+ settled at zero 99.3%+ of the time --
  so a 97% model line is the stable way to ask for 99% real safety.
