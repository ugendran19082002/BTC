  Best: skip when yesterday moved >2.0% -- but read the caveat below before using it.

  THE CAVEAT THAT MATTERS: these thresholds were chosen by looking at the
  same 733 days they are scored on. That is how a filter is found and it is
  not how one is proved. A rule fitted to the days it is tested on will
  always look better than it will behave. Section 5 splits the record.

5.  DOES ANY OF IT HOLD UP OUT OF SAMPLE?
======================================================================================
  first half  2024-09-04 to 2025-10-09   (238 days)
  second half 2025-10-10 to 2026-09-07   (238 days)

  FILTER                          1st half net  2nd half net   holds?
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
  skip when yesterday moved >2.0%          +105           +40      yes
  skip when yesterday moved >4.0%           -51           -17       NO
  skip when IV > 55%                       -20           -80       NO
  skip when yesterday moved >3.0%          -111           -94       NO
  skip Mondays                            -134          -236       NO
  skip when premium > $14                 -139          -439       NO

  A filter that helps in one half and hurts in the other has found a
  feature of those particular days, not of the strategy. The README says
  the same thing about the R&D engine: results flip between regimes, and
  only what survives both halves is worth carrying.
