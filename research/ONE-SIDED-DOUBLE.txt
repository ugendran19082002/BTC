IS IT THE PLACEMENT, OR JUST THE SIZE?
====================================================================================
                                vs LOCKED   lots vs LOCKED
  DOUBLE                           1.356x           1.262x
  ALWAYS 2                         2.000x           2.000x

  Return per lot, DOUBLE against LOCKED: 1.074x
  Above 1.00 means the extra lots did better than the average lot -- the
  one-sided leg was a better bet. Below 1.00 means they did worse and the
  gain is only the extra size, which could have been had by simply
  trading larger every day.

====================================================================================
WHAT A ONE-SIDED DAY IS WORTH, PER LOT
====================================================================================
  legs on one-sided days       261 legs   avg Rs  +13.40   win  99.2%   worst Rs     -311   PF 11.73
  legs on two-sided days       734 legs   avg Rs   +8.63   win  98.9%   worst Rs   -1,259   PF  2.46

  This is the number the whole idea rests on. If a leg sold on a one-sided
  day is worth less per lot than one sold alongside a partner, then putting
  more size on it is putting more size on the worse trade.
